TALENTFORGE  ·  BUSINESS GTM 08 / 12
The total addressable market for technical hiring is roughly $254 billion — that's India's IT services industry plus the global tech hiring market. Of that, the slice that's actually addressable by a verification layer is about $2 billion. Here's the math: 10 million technical hires a year globally, at an average revenue per verification of around $200, gives you a $2 billion SAM. Our serviceable obtainable market in three years is 1% of that — $20 million. That's a credible Year-3 target for a focused verification layer, and it leaves enormous headroom beyond. The wedge is small. The market behind it is not.
