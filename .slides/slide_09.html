Our go-to-market is built on one principle: attach, don't compete. We don't try to become a job board. We don't try to steal candidates from staffing firms. We plug in. First, staffing firms like CIEL and ProSculpt — we co-sell as an embedded step inside their existing candidate pipeline. Second, campus placement cells — we pilot inside one live drive as the post-screen filter. They already own the candidate distribution; we add signal. Third, ATS and HR-tech platforms — white-label API integration at the post-screen step. The attach point is exactly here — after the CV screen, before the engineer interview. We are the missing step. We don't compete for candidates or employers. We improve signal inside the workflow recruiters already own.
