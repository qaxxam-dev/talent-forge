TALENTFORGE  ·  BUSINESS GTM 07 / 12
Here's the one thing that makes us different from every other hiring tool: we're a layer, not a destination. First — our questions are generated from the candidate's own claimed work. You can't study for them with LeetCode. Second — we sit on top of your ATS, we don't compete with it for candidates. We return signal to the workflow you already own. Third — every verification produces an auditable seal with a verification ID, timestamp, and content hash. You can keep it, share it, or pass it on. That's our moat. The questions are personal to the work the candidate says they did — generic prep doesn't work. Defensible because the work itself is the test.
