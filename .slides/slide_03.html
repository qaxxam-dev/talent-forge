TALENTFORGE  ·  BUSINESS GTM 03 / 12
TalentForge is not another job board, not another testing platform — it's a layer. Here's the four-step story. You submit a job description and a pile of CVs. TalentForge strips PII, runs skill-gap analysis, ranks the pile, and produces a shortlist of ten to twenty candidates — that's step one. For each of those twenty, it builds a personalized three-sprint evidence plan, or does a light analysis of their public GitHub repo — that's step two. Then it asks them three targeted questions, generated from their own claimed work, that only they could answer if they actually did the work — that's step three, the code-defend viva. Finally, it returns a DPDP-compliant dossier to your ATS — score, business-impact statements, authorship seal. And throughout, AI suggests a score with rationale. The recruiter always makes the final mark.
