TALENTFORGE  ·  BUSINESS GTM 10 / 12
We take TalentForge to market in three phases over eighteen months. Each phase has one success metric — if we miss it, we don't move on. Phase one, months one to four: PILOT. We run TalentForge inside one or two live campus drives. We measure one thing — reduction in engineer interview hours. Target is thirty percent. Phase two, months five to ten: CO-SELL. We partner with one or two staffing firms — CIEL, ProSculpt — as their embedded verification step. Target is fifty drives processed and our first paid per-verification contracts. Phase three, months eleven to eighteen: INTEGRATE. White-label API into a major ATS platform. Target is one signed partnership and a million-dollar ARR run-rate. Eighteen months from a single pilot to a platform integration.
