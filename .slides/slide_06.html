Today, recruiters pick from four kinds of tools — and each one trades off something. HackerRank-style auto tests solve for volume, but they're gameable by AI and not tied to the candidate's own work. Mercer Mettl and iMocha add proctoring and integrity, but the underlying questions are still generic DSA — not a defense of real work. Generic video interviews like HireVue give you a soft signal, but they're not tied to the candidate's actual code. The manual live technical interview is the gold standard — it's the only one that actually verifies depth. But it doesn't scale past about 20 candidates. TalentForge is the only one that solves for both — volume AND a defense of the candidate's own work. We're not replacing any of these. We're the missing step between them.
