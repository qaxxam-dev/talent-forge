India's IT services industry is a $254 billion engine. It hires over 1.5 million engineers every year — a huge share of them through campus and lateral drives. That's the macro picture. But here's what changed in the last 18 months: more than half of all technical CVs now show some AI-assisted content. Three trends are converging at once. First, campus drive volumes are rising year on year as IT services rebounds from the slowdown. Second, generative AI adoption among candidates has surged since 2023, so every portfolio looks polished. Third, Day-Zero skill gaps persist after hiring because screeners can't verify real technical depth. The volume is going up. The signal is going down. That's the macro window TalentForge is built for.
